# All Steak Explained — key points (finer map)

Source: [Flue — All Steak Explained in 12 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mezHN2-qmrs)

## Ribeye & Tomahawk
- Ribs 6–12, least-worked → naturally tender; **highest intramuscular fat** of commercial steaks; marble melts and bastes from inside.
- Bone-in = cowboy; long bone (6–8 in) = **tomahawk**. Bone doesn’t add flavor — plating theater ($80–150); meat cost ≈ standard ribeye.

## Filet Mignon
- French *filet mignon* is pork; beef tenderloin is *tournedos*. US “filet mignon” popularized by **O. Henry (1906)** as luxury shorthand.
- Spine muscle that never bears weight; **~500 g** true filet per cow. Scarcity + myth = price. Most tender, **mildest** flavor (almost no fat).

## New York Strip
- Short loin / longissimus; other half of a T-bone (no tenderloin). **Delmonico’s** fame; credible **Kansas City** origin — “New York” stuck via press.
- Less fat than ribeye, firmer chew, cleaner beef — sits between filet and ribeye.

## T-Bone
- Strip + tenderloin on a T vertebra. Cook two meats at once; tenderloin overcooks fast; bone makes a heat-texture ring. Visual drama drives the premium.

## Hanger · Skirt · Flank
- **Hanger** (*onglet*): one per cow, diaphragm support; butchers kept it; ≤ medium-rare or it dries. Price rose after 1990s menus.
- **Skirt**: plate/diaphragm; vaqueros’ wage cut → fajitas; **Ninfa’s 1973** nationalized; price quadrupled by late ’80s.
- **Flank**: abdominal fibers — against the grain. “London broil” is a **method**, not a cut; prices roughly doubled 2000–2015.

## Picanha · Sirloin
- **Picanha**: Brazilian rump cap with fat triangle; name from herding spike. US trims it away; Brazilians score, C-skewer fat-out over charcoal. Steakhouse chains popularized it after 2000.
- **Sirloin**: knighting myth false → French *sur longe*. Top sirloin = strong value; bottom yields **tri-tip**.

## Flat Iron · Denver · Tri-Tip · Matsusaka
- **Flat iron**: NCBA lab project (Calkins & Johnson); infraspinatus = 2nd most tender; split out the center seam → strip-like taste, half price; iron shape; Applebee’s menus.
- **Denver**: same project, marketing name for serratus ventralis (4th most tender); Japan *zabuton*; **most marbled** muscle — still obscure.
- **Tri-tip**: Santa Maria / California; East Coast mystery for decades; cook whole; **two grain directions**.
- **Matsusaka** (Mie): virgin females only; beer/music/massage lore (mixed real/marketing); marbling past top Kobe; sweeter fat.
